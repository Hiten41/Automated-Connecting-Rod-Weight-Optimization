# Engine load validation

The module implements exact zero-offset slider-crank kinematics. With crank radius r, rod length l and angle theta, piston position from the crank center is x=r cos(theta)+sqrt(l²-r² sin²(theta)); piston acceleration follows the analytic second derivative. Rod angle satisfies sin(beta)=r/l sin(theta). Taking crankward piston acceleration a positive and compression rod force C positive, piston equilibrium is Fgas-C cos(beta)=m*a, so C=(Fgas-m*a)/cos(beta). Fgas=(pcyl-pref)piD²/4. The FEA convention assigns tension positive and thus the opposite rod-force sign.

The retained automobile-derived reference is the Ricardo Hydra gasoline research engine based on the GM2.0L Cavalier bottom end, not a stock production connecting rod. Leeds thesis reports bore85.99mm,1500RPM half-load gas/rod plots and piston assembly mass577.8g; Mufti/Priest publishes86mm stroke and143.5mm rod. Digitized plot readings are approximate with stated reading uncertainty. Sparse gas-force points were converted to differential pressure and then back to gas force as a unit check, not independent combustion validation. The separately read rod-force curve comparison MAE is412.61N. Published piston inertia readings and exact kinematic acceleration agree within visual plot resolution.

Engine sources: [Mufti Leeds thesis](https://etheses.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/5354/1/414535.pdf) and [Mufti & Priest, ASME DOI10.1115/1.3176988](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.3176988). A further search found a Hydra compression-ignition configuration with different79mm bore/160mm rod in [Ristow Hadlich et al.](https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/you.stonybrook.edu/dist/e/1841/files/2024/11/RistowHadlich_et_al_MachineLearning_GTP-23-1668_AuthorProof.pdf); those parameters are not mixed with the Leeds gasoline engine. No matching raw pressure data were obtained.

Optional additional rod mass uses a two-end lumping. For rod mass mr and center-of-mass distance fraction f from the crank pin, equivalent piston-end mass is mr*f, rotating crank-end mass mr*(1-f). The CAD center of mass can supply an illustrative uniform-density f. It does not preserve a general rod inertia tensor or distributed bending and no measured reference rod mass/CG is available. The published piston-assembly preset remains unchanged; additional mass defaults to zero, preventing double counting.

Design requirements remain28kN compression and18kN tension. These are project design loads, not a measured Hydra operating envelope. The small1500RPM half-load literature example is not substituted for them. The assumed material yield is710MPa pending actual heat-treatment certificate. Engine calculations are illustrative and do not validate combustion loading experimentally.
