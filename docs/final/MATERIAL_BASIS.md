# Material basis

All Phase3 reference-design comparisons retain E=205000MPa, Poisson ratio0.29, density7.85e-6kg/mm3, yield710MPa, required FoS1.8 and allowable394.444MPa. The displacement limit remains0.35mm.

The 710MPa yield value remains a project assumption: no material certificate or specific forging/heat-treatment specification supports that exact value. Atlas Steels4340 supplier data lists quenched-and-tempered bar proof strengths dependent on section and supply condition, including835MPa for diameter<=60mm,740MPa for60–100mm,720MPa for100–178mm, and690MPa for178–240mm. This confirms heat-treatment and section dependence; it does not certify the proposed rod or justify silently raising its allowable stress.

Source: [Atlas4340 grade sheet, revised10 September2021](https://atlassteels.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Through-Hardening-Low-Alloy-Steel-Bar-4340-Grade-Data-Sheet-10-09-21-1.pdf), page1. Forging quality, local heat treatment, residual stress, fatigue strength and inspection remain unqualified. No material substitution is used to manufacture a passing optimization result.
