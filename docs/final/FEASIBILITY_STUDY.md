# Feasibility study

## Scope and method

A seeded 40-point Latin hypercube sampled six supported CAD variables: ring thickness, shank width, beam thickness, pocket depth, flange thickness, and big-end transition radius. Each record retains the proposed parameters, FreeCAD geometry check, actual CAD mass, analytical screening, and separate compression/tension cosine-bearing FEA where CAD succeeded. The fixed envelope is 145 mm center distance, 50/24 mm bores, 4340 model material, 28 kN compression, 18 kN tension, yield assumption 710 MPa, FoS 1.8, stress limit 394.444 MPa, and displacement limit 0.35 mm. Invalid geometries and solver outcomes are retained in the study files.

## Results

- 40 distinct geometries were attempted.
- 35 produced valid CAD and completed both 6 mm local FEA load cases.
- Five failed CAD/geometry validation; none were silently dropped.
- No sampled geometry passed the coarse stress screen. Displacement remained below its 0.35 mm limit in the valid screening records.
- The closest candidate by maximum stress ratio was case 4, mass 690.247 g. Its coarse 6 mm stresses were 323.140 MPa compression and 409.298 MPa tension. It was refined at 0.75 mm for both loads: 374.866 MPa compression and 501.358 MPa tension, with 0.09355/0.14774 mm displacement. At that mesh its tension FoS is 1.416, below 1.8. It is rejected.
- Candidate 4's CAD mass is about 6.1% below the baseline mass, but the candidate fails tension and does not count as an optimized design or an accepted weight reduction.

## Exploratory variable associations

Across the 35 valid geometries, unadjusted Spearman rank association with coarse tension stress is strongest for ring thickness (ρ = −0.809), followed by transition radius (ρ = −0.453), beam thickness (ρ = −0.443), and shank width (ρ = −0.204). In this sample ring thickness is also strongly associated with lower tension displacement (ρ = −0.937). Beam thickness has the strongest association with mass (ρ = 0.754). These are exploratory marginal correlations: variables co-vary in the Latin hypercube, the study is small, and these statistics do not establish causal effects or a global response surface. Full values and unadjusted p-values are in `data/phase3/feasibility/sensitivity.json`.

The actual-CAD mass/stress, mass/displacement, compression/tension, constraint-margin, and variable-versus-tension views are generated by `scripts/phase3_feasibility_report.py` under `evidence/phase3/`. Hover data identify candidate indices. All plots are explicitly coarse-screening results.

## Conclusion and limits

The result is **INCONCLUSIVE: no feasible candidate was found in the 40 sampled geometries**. The nearest sampled candidate still fails the tension requirement after 0.75 mm refinement. This does not prove that every point in the bounded design space is infeasible; the space is sparsely sampled and only one near-boundary candidate was refined. The final lightweight-optimization objective remains unmet. Further search should emphasize larger ring thickness and transition radius while preserving CAD and manufacturing constraints, then verify candidates on paired finer meshes. The 710 MPa yield value remains a model assumption pending material certification.
